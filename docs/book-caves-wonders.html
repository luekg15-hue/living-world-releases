Wonders

Glow-worm grottos

Some cave roofs are hung with glow-worm silk, beaded with soft blue light. Run about and they go dark; keep quiet and they come back.

Glow-Worm SilkGlow-Worm Lantern

Shear silk for a lantern: bottle, silk, stick.

Hot springs

Pools of warm water deep down. A soak warms you right through. Drop in herbs or honey for an herbal bath:

SteepBath gives
Lavenderserenity
Chamomileregeneration
Mintwater breathing
Nettleresistance
Honeyabsorption

Singing crystals

Pale crystal glowing in deep hollows. Brush past and each rings its own note.

→

Their shards make wind chimes.

Underground rivers

Long dark rivers wind through the rock, deep enough for a boat, with a ledge to walk on.

Oak BoatBlind Cave FishCooked Cave Fish

Blind cave fish bite there.

Cave paintings

Old figures in ochre on the walls. Put paper to one to take a rubbing.

Painted StonePaperRubbing of a Cave Painting

Eight figures to find.

Secret The hidden pots (click to reveal)

The painters hid a pot in the rock behind each group. Read a rubbing to learn which way and how far.

→

Find all eight figures for the painters' knack.

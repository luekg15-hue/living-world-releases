Naturalist's Field Guide

Deer and genes

Deer

Shy herds of the woods and meadows. Walk up openly and they flag their white tails and bolt. Sneak, or hold out apples or berries, and they let you near.

Genes

Every deer carries genes from both parents. A fawn is a blend of the two, give or take a little, so over the generations you can breed a herd towards what you want.

Hold apples or berries and look at a deer to read it:

Stag · Brown · Large · 8-point · Wary · carries Piebald

Coat

Five shades, light tan to dark chocolate. A fawn's coat falls between its parents'.

In winter every coat turns greyer and thicker.

Antlers

A stag's rack runs from a single spike to ten points, and bigger with every step. Does carry the gene too, and pass it on.

Antlers are shed in winter and regrow through spring.

Size

From small (85%) to huge (115%). A large deer gives an extra venison, a huge one two.

Temperament

How close a deer lets you come before it bolts:

Skittish: notices you 20 blocks off, bolts at 11.

Wary: somewhere between.

Calm: notices you at 10, bolts only at 4.

Wild deer are mostly skittish. Breed calm ones together for a herd that is easy to work with.

Rare coats

Three rare coats run in families:

Wild odds: piebald 1 in 50, melanistic 1 in 120, leucistic 1 in 200.

Now and then a fawn gets a new copy, so no rare coat ever dies out.

Hidden genes

Each deer has two copies of a rare gene, one from each parent. Two copies: it shows. One: a hidden carrier.

P: a piebald copy. Two carriers: 1 fawn in 4 is piebald.

Rare coat odds

Carrier × carrier: 1 fawn in 4 shows it, 2 in 4 carry it.

Shows × shows: every fawn shows it.

Shows × carrier: half show it, the rest carry it.

Carrier × clear: none show it, but half carry it.

Breeding tips

Each gene is passed on by itself: breed a calm herd and a trophy line at the same time.

Wild herds are families. Find one rare deer and its herdmates likely carry the gene too.

Apples or berries put deer in the mood. A feeding trough of apples keeps a herd breeding by itself.

A friend for life

No deer can resist a heartberry cake: one of every wild berry. Feed it to a grown deer and it's yours.

A tame deer fears no one. Another cake heals it.

Riding deer

Put a deer saddle on your tame deer and ride. Hold jump to gather a leap: a deer bounds higher and further than any horse.

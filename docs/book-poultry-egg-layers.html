Egg layers

Leghorn

Egg layer

White and light, with a big red comb. The best layer of all: eggs nearly every day.

Eggs: many, white. Size: few.

Isa Brown

Egg layer

A friendly brown hybrid, bred on farms for steady laying.

Eggs: many, brown. Size: some.

Ameraucana

Egg layer

Lavender-blue, with a fluffy beard and cheek muffs. It lays sky-blue eggs.

Eggs: many, blue. Size: some.

Marans

Egg layer

Black with copper hackles. It lays the darkest eggs of all: deep chocolate brown.

Eggs: many, chocolate. Size: some.

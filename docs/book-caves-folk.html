Folk

Secret The knockers (click to reveal)

Knocking in the rock? Drop a pasty. They'll knock from the best ore near you for a while: dig towards it.

Friends of theirs hear frantic knocking before breaking into lava.

Turn on subtitles to hear which way.

Secret The pasty (click to reveal)
→2
→ in a Furnace

Beef or mutton. Bake in a furnace, smoker or campfire.

Secret The Goblin Market (click to reveal)

On a new moon night, goblins set out stalls somewhere in the dark near you, selling for gold nuggets.

Gold NuggetGoblin Fruit

Goblin fruit is wonderful. You'll want another.

Secret The bridge troll (click to reveal)

A troll guards a bridge over a chasm. He wants food to let you by.

Feed him well and he'll call you friend.

He's very fond of pasties.

The Seed Saver's Handbook

Genes at work

Yield and vigour

Yield sets the harvest: from 0.6 to 1.4 times an ordinary plant's.

Vigour: genes 13 and 11 → shows as 12.0

Weak plants dawdle; vigorous ones grow up to a third faster.

Hardiness

Hardy plants bear dry soil and the wrong season better.

The hardiest grow on through winter now and then, and shrug off killing frost.

Breed it for early and late sowing.

Size and flavour

Their average grades the produce:

GradeNeedsFood
Fine9½++1 hunger
Choice11++1, filling
Prize13++2, filling

Ordinary produce isn't graded.

At the fair

Graded produce scores in the fruit and veg basket: 3 marks a grade.

A giant pumpkin's size gene sets how fast it swells: half to half again as fast.

Save seed from your champion.

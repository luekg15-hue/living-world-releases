The Seed Saver's Handbook

Saving seed

Going to seed

Leave a ripe plant standing most of a day and it goes to seed: fluff drifts from it.

Harvest it then for saved seed: its own genes and its pollen father's. More seed, less crop.

Saved seed may not breed true.

Ordinary harvests

Pick a ripe plant before it goes to seed and you get its crop, and the packet you sowed back, unchanged.

So a field sown from one packet gives back seed that stacks.

Choosing parents

Save from your best plants and the line creeps up each year. Save from the worst and it slides back.

Look over each plant before harvest, or compare packets in a tin.

Only the best few go to seed.

The seed tin

Holds nine packets side by side, a bar chart under each. Gold marks the best.

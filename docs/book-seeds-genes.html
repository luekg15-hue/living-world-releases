The Seed Saver's Handbook

Genes

Five traits

TraitWhat it does
Yieldhow much you harvest
Vigourhow fast it grows
Hardinessfrost, drought, winter
Flavourhow good to eat
Sizehow big it grows

Each runs from 0 to 15. An ordinary plant is about 7 or 8.

Two copies

Every gene comes in two copies, one from each parent. The plant shows the average of the two.

Yield: genes 12 and 4 → shows as 8.0

This plant looks ordinary, yet carries a fine copy it can pass on.

Reading a packet

The bars show what its seedlings will show, on average. Gen counts how often the line has been saved.

Breeds true: five dots and every seedling comes out alike. Fewer, and they scatter.

Hover over a packet to read it.

Reading a plant

Hold any seed and look at a crop: its genes show above your hotbar.

It says too whether the plant has gone to seed.

Walk your rows before harvest and note your best.

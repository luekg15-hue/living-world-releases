The Border Collie

The Border Collie

Strays turn up in sheep country: plains, meadows, windswept hills. Wary, but meat wins them over, mutton best of all.

Bandanas and Pups

A scrap of woollen cloth makes a working bandana (shears take it off); any dye colours the collar. Two tame collies fed meat have pups, fluffy and dark till they grow.

Fetching the Flock

Send your collies with a shepherd's crook and they fetch the flock to you: out wide round the far side, then low and slow up behind, dropping to the eye if they get too close. Sheep, goats, cattle, ducks or geese: whichever are nearest.

A sitting collie stays sat.

The Shepherd's Crook

→

Use it on the ground: "come bye", and they bring the flock there. Use it in the air: "that'll do".

Keeping a Collie

A tame collie sits and follows like any dog. It sees off foxes and wild wolves, chases crows off the crops, and goes for anything that hurts you.

Hold out meat to a sitting collie and it begs.

Collie Habits

A happy collie wags; a working one keeps its tail low and its eyes on the sheep. It tilts its head at you, play-bows to its owner, and shakes itself dry after a swim.

Told to sit, it lies down after a while, and curls up to sleep at night.

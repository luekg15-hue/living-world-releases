The Undergrove

The Undergrove

No seasons reach this deep, so the cave plants keep the moon's time. Each roots in its own ground, never bare stone.

Lantern BulbDarkbloomBreathbellGoblin's PurseStarmoss

Silt by the rivers, guano under the bats, root mat where the trees come through.

By the moon

Count the nights on a calendar: eight to a month, full moon to full moon.

MoonBelow
FullBulbs swell; dripwort runs
WaningMoonwood dims
NewPurse goes gold; nectar
WaxingMoonwood brightens

Lanternroot

Roots of the trees above, through the roof. The bulbs glow the tree's colour, ripest at the full moon.

→

Plant a bulb against root mat; crouch to hang it as a lamp.

Breathbell

Pale bells that grow in and over cave water, mouth down, each holding a pocket of air. Swim into a full one for a breath.

BreathbellCave Silt

An emptied bell fills again slowly, bubble by bubble.

Darkbloom

Opens only in total darkness. A torch near it, or a light in your hand, and it snaps shut.

DarkbloomDarkbloom Nectar

Put your light out and wait. Bottle the nectar of an open flower: you'll see in the dark.

Dripwort

A pale vine on damp roofs. Its tip beads with stone-water, fastest at the full moon.

DripwortStone-Water

Catch the bead in a bottle, or set a cauldron under it.

Goblin's purse

Little caps stacked like coins on guano and old roots. They turn gold only in the dark of the moon.

Goblin's PurseGuano Heap

The goblins take one gold cap for three nuggets.

Hushmoss

A soft grey moss that swallows sound. Walk on it and the sculk never hears you.

→3

Thick in the Deep Dark. It creeps across dark floors.

Embermoss

Charcoal moss on rock near lava, its tips smouldering. Stand by it and you warm through.

EmbermossDried EmbermossEmber Tea

Dry it in a smoker or over a campfire: fuel twice as long as coal, or ember tea in a teapot.

Starmoss

Dark moss on high cavern roofs, pricked with points of light. They come out only while it's night above.

Starmoss

They twinkle most at the full moon.

Bats and snails

Bats come to darkbloom and goblin's purse and carry their seed away to grow somewhere new. Snails graze them.

GuanoGuano Heap

Guano from a bat roost feeds any cave plant on a step.

Dark beds

Grow darkbloom and goblin's purse at home: a dark bed in a cellar, out of the light, with water near.

→2

Lanternroot and dripwort want a roof: a ceiling of root mat.

The Undergrove

Rare and deep: a great cavern where all of them grow together, round a still pool.

Moonwood LogLantern BulbHushmoss

Follow the light. Bats know the way.

Moonwood

The pale tree in the pool. Its veins glow with the moon: bright at the full, dark at the new.

Moonwood LogMoonwood Branch

Its wood keeps the moon's time wherever you build with it.

The moon dial

Its window shows tonight's moon. Use it to read what the cave plants are doing.

→

Starmoss, a moonwood branch and a gold ingot.

More uses

Bottle a full breathbell for air to carry. Pour stone-water on a crop to grow it. Crouch and use hushmoss to line your boots: your steps go quiet.

Bottled AirStone-WaterHushmossToasted Goblin's Purse

Nectar and breathbells brew into potions. Knockers take a gold cap like a pasty.

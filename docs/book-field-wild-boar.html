Wild boar

Wild boar

Tusked pigs of the deep woods. Come within 10 blocks and they charge; hurt a piglet and the whole sounder comes for you.

Taming a boar

Hold an apple or a carrot and they calm down and follow it.

Feed one: smoke means not yet, hearts mean it is yours (about 1 time in 3).

A tame boar follows you, sits when you right-click it, guards you and breeds. Its piglets are tame too.

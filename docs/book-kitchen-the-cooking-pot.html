The Cook's Companion

The cooking pot

Simmering

Fill the pot with a water bucket. Add up to six ingredients, one at a time.

Over heat it simmers. Then ladle it out with a bowl.

Use the pot with an empty hand to look inside.

Pottage

If what's in the pot isn't a recipe, you get pottage. Filling, nothing special.

The pot's screen shows what it'll make.

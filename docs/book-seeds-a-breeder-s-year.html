The Seed Saver's Handbook

A breeder's year

A first project

1. Sow common carrot seed in a bed.

2. Look over each plant; note the biggest and tastiest.

3. Let only those go to seed, near your hives.

...and on

4. Sow their seed in a bed of its own, well apart.

5. Save from the best again, every year.

6. In three or four years it breeds true, and takes a name.

Then cross it with an heirloom.

Tips

Keep a seed tin for each crop.

Name a fine line at an anvil.

Raised beds keep lines apart.

A clone keeps a perfect plant for good.

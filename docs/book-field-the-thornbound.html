The Thornbound

The Grimoire

The pillagers guard a black book bound in iron brambles. Woodsmen say the illagers stole it, long ago, from something older and worse than themselves. It whispers to whoever carries it, and it is hungry.

Thornbound Grimoire

Look in the chests of a pillager outpost.

Spells, and their price

Mark a page with the ribbon and use the book to cast it; crouch to read it. Every spell asks a price: the life in the grass and flowers, a soul it holds, your own blood, or worse.

Nothing in the book is free.

Pencil, ink and gold

Pages start as runes. The book sets riddles in its margins; answer one with a deed and the runes become a spell in pencil. Cast it on many days (once a day counts) and the book asks for something harder: answer, and the page is inked, then illuminated in gold.

Torn pages

Pages have been torn from the book and carried off. Find one and use it with the grimoire on you to bind it back in.

Torn Page: Soul EntrapmentTorn Page: SoulflameTorn Page: StormcallTorn Page: The Swarm

One went with the evokers, to their mansions. One burns in the Nether's fortresses. The sea took one. The wild bees keep the last.

The druid coven

The book's first owners want it back. On full-moon nights they hunt whoever has held it, in forest and taiga: three to five druids, led by a Stormcaller.

Thornwarden, Hivecaller and Stormcaller.

Their ways

They keep their distance and cast the book's own spells. The Thornwarden raises thorns around you, the Hivecaller wakes the flowers into angry bees, and the Stormcaller's lightning falls where it marked you a moment before. Keep moving.

When they come

Some time between dusk and midnight. The forest goes quiet first; listen for the chant, for they will rise where it comes from.

At sunrise, any still standing sink back into the moss.

What they leave

Every druid carries Coven Ink. Drink it with the grimoire on you and the page under its ribbon takes a day's practice. Now and then one drops its pointed hat.

Coven InkThornwarden's CrownHivecaller's SkepStormcaller's Spire

The hats can be worn.

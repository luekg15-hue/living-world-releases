Naturalist's Field Guide

Livestock

Livestock genes

Cows, pigs and chickens pass on genes like deer: a blend of both parents.

Size (more meat), growth (young grow faster) and fertility (shorter rest, twins).

Each kind's gift

Hens: laying. The best lay eggs half as often again.

Pigs: litters. Twins up to half the time, and triplets.

Cows: hide. Thick-hided cows give extra leather.

Crops & herbs

Every crop in Wildhearth, and Minecraft's own, grows as a 3D plant with its own look at every stage, from the first seed leaves to the harvest. Grown plants come in a few shapes and turns, so a field never looks copy-pasted.

The kitchen gardenUnder glassMinecraft's ownHerbs

The kitchen garden

Sown outdoors from spring, each in its season (see the almanac).

Beans

Broad beans: square stems with paired leaves, white flowers with dark blotches, then plump pods hanging in twos and threes as the lower leaves yellow.

Beans

Cabbages

Waxy blue-green leaves cup up into a rosette, a pale heart forms, and the ripe head sits layered inside big outer leaves curling back.

CabbageCabbage Seeds

Onions

Hollow tube leaves fan up and the bulb swells at the surface. Ripe, the tops flop over and yellow, golden bulbs half out of the soil.

Onion

Garlic

A fan of flat strap leaves and a curling scape, then papery white bulbs with purple streaks as the leaves brown from the tips.

GarlicGarlic Clove

Turnips

Rough lobed leaves arch over a white root that shows its purple shoulders above the soil.

TurnipTurnip Seeds

Sweet potatoes

Vines creep over the soil with heart-shaped leaves on stalks. Ripe, reddish tubers lie half-buried among them.

Sweet PotatoSweet Potato Slips

Chillies

A small bush of narrow pointed leaves, white flowers, then slim pods hanging below the leaves, red, orange and green.

ChilliChilli Seeds

Strawberries

Three-part leaves fan from a crown, white flowers open, and berries hang at the edges, cream-white first and red when ripe. Runners root new plants beside them.

StrawberriesStrawberry Plant

Tomatoes

A bushy young plant, then a tall vine tied up its stake: yellow star flowers, trusses of pale fruit with an orange blush, then glossy red.

TomatoTomato Seeds

Squash

Big lobed leaves stand up on their stalks and runners creep out across the ground. The vines flower, and the flowers swell into ribbed butternuts.

Butternut SquashSquash Seeds

Corn

Two blocks tall: long tapering leaves, a tassel on top, and cobs with silk tufts that turn golden, the husk peeled back.

Corn Cob

Under glass

The warm-country crops: best in a heated glasshouse.

Sweet peppers

A sturdy glossy bush with white star flowers, then lobed bell peppers that hang green and ripen red.

Sweet PepperSweet Pepper Seeds

Aubergines

A broad, felty bush on purple stems with purple star flowers, then glossy teardrop aubergines under spiky green caps.

AubergineAubergine Seeds

Cucumbers

Climbs a bamboo cane wigwam: broad rough leaves, big yellow flowers, then long pale striped cucumbers hanging in the corners.

CucumberCucumber Seeds

Grapevine

A gnarled trunk climbs its post and spreads a canopy of vine leaves. Bunches hang below, green and then purple. Pick them and it fruits again.

GrapesGrapevine Cutting

Minecraft's own

Wildhearth redraws the game's own crops in 3D too. They grow and harvest exactly as before.

Wheat

Fine shoots thicken into a stand of stalks. The ears swell green, then the whole stand turns gold, bearded ears nodding at different heights.

WheatWheat Seeds

Carrots

Feathery fronds rise from a crown while the orange shoulders of the roots push up out of the soil.

Carrot

Potatoes

A bushy plant of compound leaves with white star flowers. Ripe, the haulm yellows and falls over pale tubers peeking from the soil.

Potato

Beetroot

Glossy leaves with red stems and veins over a fat red-purple root.

BeetrootBeetroot Seeds

Pumpkins

Broad rough leaves, a vine with a curly tendril and a big trumpet flower, then the vine arches over into its pumpkin.

Pumpkin

Melons

Like the pumpkin but slighter, with deeply fingered blue-green leaves and small star flowers.

Nether wart

Red nubs push up through the soul sand and grow into a crowded clump of plump, glossy crimson warts.

Cocoa

Ribbed pods on a short stalk from the jungle bark: green, then ochre, then a ripe deep orange-brown.

Cocoa Beans

Sweet berries

A low sprig grows into a round bush of dark leaves on thorny twigs, hung with clusters of red berries.

Sweet Berries

Herbs

Wild herbs grow in patches in meadows and woods, and lavender spreads in a bed. Each comes in three shapes, turned at random. More in the Field Guide.

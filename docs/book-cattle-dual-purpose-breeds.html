Dual-purpose breeds

Simmental

Dual-purpose

Gold-red with a white face and patches. Good for both milk and beef.

Milk again: 2½ min. Horns: short.

Shorthorn

Dual-purpose

Red roan with long horns. Dual-purpose, with a good thick hide.

Milk again: 2½ min. Horns: long.

South Devon

Dual-purpose

Big, gentle and light red, without horns. Dual-purpose and large.

Milk again: 3 min. Horns: mostly polled.

Dual at a glance

A bit of both: good size and middling milk.

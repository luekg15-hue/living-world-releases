The almanac

What the land is doing through the year: a whole Wildhearth year at a glance, from the game's own calendars. Each season is split into early, mid and late.

  • Spring Blossom time: the orchard needs its bees; sow most crops.
  • Summer Hay and berries; Midsummer Eve; cherries, peaches and plums ripen.
  • Autumn Apples and pears; the Harvest Fair; All Hallows Eve; leaves turn.
  • Winter Snow and frost; crops rest; citrus ripens; mead ages in the cellar.
